Advancing

Each sight is advanced from its own time to the fix time using its own course, speed, set and drift, so an earlier sight moves further. Set these per sight below.

Defaults for new sights

Sea horizon is the starting setting. Artificial horizon halves Hs with no dip; a nearer waterline uses its distance and eye height for short-horizon dip. Index error on arc subtracts; off arc adds. Each page loads saved DR at startup and keeps its own later edits. Set/drift advance LOPs for running fixes.

Planetarium & star planner

Smaller magnitudes mean brighter stars. Morning/evening buttons find the next Sun altitude of −8° within 48 hours. These are planning times; clouds, haze and the visible horizon still matter.

Choose a DR and UTC time.

Sky above your horizon

Looking up: north at the top, east on the left. Rim = horizon, centre = zenith. Gold stars form the suggested set; muted stars are outside your filters. Sun = yellow disc, Moon = pale disc, planets = orange rings. Click an object for details.

Suggested stars

The planner favours well-spaced true bearings, then brighter stars and moderate altitudes. Its geometry score describes bearing spread, not expected fix accuracy. Altitudes are geocentric geometric Hc. Refraction, lunar parallax, limb and dip corrections belong to sight reduction; the plotted discs are symbolic, not to scale.

Sun, Moon & planets

All seven are listed; only objects above the horizon are plotted. These markers are independent of the star filters and suggested star set.
